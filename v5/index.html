Our Legacy

35 Years of Purity & Trust

For over three and a half decades, Pushpamangalam Gold & Diamonds (By Saju Pattara & Sons) has been a cherished part of milestones and wedding traditions across Alappuzha. What began as a trusted family jewellery house in Mannancherry grew through generations of families who returned for our unyielding promise of honest purity, fair making charges, and personal care.

Today, we carry that same heritage into our welcoming new flagship showroom in Cherthala, opposite Devi Kshetram Municipal Complex—bringing together traditional Kerala artistry, lightweight modern gold, and IGI-certified diamonds under one roof.

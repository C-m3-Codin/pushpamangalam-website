Our Legacy

35 Years of Purity & Trust

For over 35 years, Pushpamangalam Gold & Diamonds (By Saju Pattara & Sons) has been a trusted name across Alappuzha's weddings and family milestones. Rooted in Mannancherry, our reputation grew through generations who valued uncompromised purity, honest making charges, and genuine personal care.

Today, that same heritage welcomes you at our new sunlit flagship showroom in Cherthala, opposite Devi Kshetram Municipal Complex—curated for unhurried browsing across traditional Kerala gold, lightweight everyday collections, and IGI-certified diamonds.
